# Independent verification of the COMP0123 overnight-network report

The main empirical results reproduce. The district-to-municipality rebuild agrees with the three report backbones edge for edge. The independent Poisson fits recover the summer increases for pre-1981 arrivals and village-born residents, the decrease for recent arrivals, and the expected signs in every one of the 117 summer–February pairs for those three terms. The emptied-zone reversal also reproduces. I found presentation errors in the calendar count, the distance-threshold sensitivity, the All Saints BOSAM percentage, the diameter row and an ECEPOV missingness note. The more consequential edits are to describe the roots result as a city-to-province association, and to distinguish the large share of the assortativity shift captured by the degree null from the statistically detectable gap that remains.

## Data reconstruction

I read the district-level 2024 extract `data/derived/pernoct_2024_6m.parquet` and the MITMA zonification CSVs independently. This is the earliest available local 2024 mobility input I found. It is a parquet conversion of the downloads, so this verifies the district mapping and all subsequent arithmetic, with the original daily download conversion out of scope. The raw crosswalk maps each district to one municipal zone after identical pairs are de-duplicated. I excluded same-zone stays and used both endpoints on the mainland. I accumulated weights to the input precision of 0.001 person before applying the cut. No pipeline functions were imported and no additional packages were installed.

## Headline estimation checks

I formed city × destination-province outcomes by summing the independently rebuilt off-diagonal stays, including zero-flow cells until their whole origin-province × destination-province fixed-effect group had zero flow. R2 uses the cleaned census city and birthplace tables named in its methods. V1 uses the 2024 INE census person extract, the ADRH income file and the independently joined geographic controls. Both fits used explicit sparse fixed effects, a Newton solver for the Poisson likelihood, log distance and squared log distance and a city-income slope for each destination province. V1 adds 0.5 to sample counts before taking logs. Since the city fixed effect absorbs the log denominator, logged counts and logged shares give the same slopes in this specification. I refitted every 2024 sister night for the point contrasts. Jackknife results are independent refits. Inference uses a separate 9,999-draw Webb bootstrap with a new random seed, so numerical p-values and inverted confidence limits can differ slightly. The summer–February levels reported as 0.532 and 0.629 are individually rounded before subtraction. Their full-precision difference is +0.09636.

## Mismatches ranked by effect on the report

1. **The design cannot trace a traveller back to a family village.** Figure 3 and the narrative correctly identify a stronger association between city-to-inland-province stays and birthplace composition. The regressors describe fellow residents, and the MITMA records provide aggregate flows. Neither observes the sleeper's own birthplace, parents, personal migration route or accommodation. `VARANOP` is arrival in the current province according to the INE census dictionary, so “generation of the rural exodus” is a historical interpretation. The separate village-born regressor uses birth-municipality size and supplies no village-level destination match in Figure 3. Explicitly state origin-province × destination-province fixed effects in the caption. The age estimate tests a particular age/cohort specification. Its interval permits a small effect. The coastline conclusion should describe where the increase is detectable. It does not follow from separate regressions that the coastal coefficient is exactly zero or that the two sample estimates sum to the all-destination estimate. ## Definitions and fragile interpretations

- **Graph denominator.** The full population of nodes is 2,435. The path length, diameter and log-based path reference use the giant component. Density and mean degree are based on the full node set. The directed medians describe the original arc directions. Table 1's “undirected projection except reciprocity” should also exclude the in-degree and out-degree row. Its N row gives only the giant-component size. The connected random graph path approximation is a heuristic. An ER graph with observed N and L gives a separate directly simulated path length. [Table 1:5,10,16–19](../reports/tables/table1.md#L5)
- **Threshold and exposure.** The observed graph changes sharply when real flows cross the detection and reporting threshold of 10. The equal-edge variant changes what fraction of travellers are represented. It also retains different undirected edge counts, because reciprocity differs between dates. Describe both dimensions in any comparison of sensitivity. Table totals count moves between the study's zones, so travel within one zone, mainland–island trips and foreign travel are outside those totals. MITMA weights are estimates with fractional precision. [Table A1:6–10,33](../reports/tables/tableA1.md#L6)
- **Equal-edge sorting.** Exact decimal accumulation and the nominal weight-then-endpoint ordering disagree with the saved sister edge lists on one-for-one cutoff-tie swaps on Feb 20 (9.591), Feb 27 (10.098) and Aug 12 (28.682). Float-sum differences below 4×10⁻¹⁵ can precede the intended endpoint tie-break. All 25 edge totals agree to input precision, and no main-night metric or displayed dagger changes. [Sort implementation](../pipeline/b00_common.py#L216) · [Differences](verify_scratch/equal_sisters.log)
- **Rich-club clique.** This is the longest completely connected initial segment of a degree ranking, with node index breaking ties. Ordinary / August / All Saints / August equal-edge values are 17 / 17 / 22 / 9 under that definition. An exact maximum clique inside the top 50 gives 25 / 41 / 37 / 27. The summer comment about a dagger has a tie on the main comparison. Across the 117 sister pairs, 58 have a smaller clique prefix in summer, 11 tie, and 48 have a larger prefix. The main summer clustering decrease holds in 91/117 pairs. The w10 summer diameter falls in 113/117 pairs and ties in four. All Saints w10 diameter ties in six pairs and falls in 33. In equal-edge summer, 27 diameter pairs tie, 88 fall and two rise. The equal-edge prefix falls in 104 of 117 pairs. Equal-edge φ50 rises in 114 of 117. [Table A1:20–29](../reports/tables/tableA1.md#L20) · [Clique definition](../pipeline/b02_zhou.py#L539)
- **Range means reachable witnesses.** Two independently seeded greedy switch searches per condition stayed within a single edge-bearing component at saved checkpoints and returned connected witnesses at least as extreme as the reported feasible endpoints, at unrounded precision. Random trials use four distinct endpoints, degree-preserving simple switches, and 900 attempted switches per observed link. There is no certificate of a global minimum or maximum. The phrase “the range that any network with the same degrees can reach” needs the heuristic qualifier. Even without an extremality proof, the degree patterns strongly constrain the observed r and the null shows most of the between-night shift. [Figure 2 caption:13](../reports/figures/captions.md#L13)
- **Second-order mixing.** The reproduced statistic takes excess degrees of remaining neighbours, removes the partner endpoint, and assigns zero average when that leaves an empty set. Empty-set treatment matters at the third decimal on the ordinary night. Dropping all edges touching a degree-one node gives −.00144 instead of +.00024. Reading the denominator in the paper as the original full node degree gives a different convention. Report the estimator actually used. The restricted null fixes the degree-threshold φ(k) curve, which need not hold a rank-threshold curve fixed inside degree ties. August R_avg ≈ −.289 versus −.252, so the sign and magnitude of the remaining gap are stable in the independent null. [Zhou, Cox and Hansen, section 2.1](https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.0687) · [Implementation:333–344](../pipeline/b02_zhou.py#L333)
- **Geography and date.** “Coastal” means the destination zone polygon touches the sea. It cannot classify whether a particular sleeper used a beach, and inland status cannot classify whether a trip was motivated by family. The file date to evening date rule is the supplied empirical convention. Use it consistently for months, weekday filters and the nine August pairs. “All year” is stronger than the data coverage, which samples six partly observed months in 2024. [Configuration](../pipeline/config.py) · [Narrative:6–13](../reports/estructura_relato.md#L6)
- **Dependence and baselines.** The 117 contrasts use only 9 + 13 unique nights. Their sign agreement is a sensitivity check, with a dependent grid. The main August all-destination R2 increase is weak under province clustering despite the 116/117 positive point contrasts, and its p-value changes substantially when the baseline is switched to October. The two exact 117/117 statements in the story should be attached to named outcomes and terms.
- **Sizes and sampling.** Across six census pseudo-count and transformation variants, the pre-1981 and village coefficients always rise and the recent coefficient always falls. On the variants that log a smoothed count or share, the pre-1981 difference ranges from +.017 to +.113 and the village difference from +.028 to +.100. The change in magnitude is substantial because the 10% sample contains many zero city–province–cohort counts. Estimated slopes refer to the chosen transformed counts. Phrasing like “tripled” amplifies noise in a small ordinary-night coefficient. Node-level inference also depends on the MITMA aggregation. R1 reproduces a relative gain in inbound links alongside a negative change in their absolute number, and the summer inflow gain weakens among single-municipality zones.
- **The date comparison and the mechanism are observational.** The adjusted August slope for log outflow per resident is −.724, and the corresponding visitor level is −.053. The latter is near zero across comparable zones. This supports different patterns for arrivals and departures. There is no decomposition of individual household choices to stay home or travel. The same concern applies to reading the 48% same-province share at Vélez as evidence of Christmas family visits.
