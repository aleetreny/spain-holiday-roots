# Final review for EPJ Data Science

**Verdict.** The revised manuscript is ready for the author's scientific sign-off and can accompany a request for UCL APC approval as a draft. I would hold journal submission until the author resolves Deviation 2, approves the declarations and confirms a versioned replication deposit. The held-out August result passes the registered rule. The most serious remaining scientific limitation is that almost the entire 2025 reference uses the earlier MITMA method.

I checked **255 numerical entries** in the four quantitative tables and audited the prose, design and figure quantities below. The count is of displayed table entries at their printed precision, with separate counts for estimates, limits and p-values. Recurrences in prose and design dates are traced below without adding them to that count. I logged **24 corrections or groups of related corrections**. The 63 analysis CSVs and ten plotted-data CSVs were read as tables. Checks of individual values target the published claims and the figures; they do not independently refit the analyses. The evidence is from journal snapshot **b71798c** and the coursework results in the review branch's base. I did not resolve discrepancies by changing the evidence.
