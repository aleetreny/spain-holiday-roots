### 1.2 Defensible Novelty Statement

Across the computational mobility, complex-network, transport-geography, and tourism/migration literatures, **no prior study links a fine-grained national mobile-phone holiday-versus-ordinary mobility network to historical census birthplace cohorts and parental origins while enforcing both econometric spatial controls and degree-constrained network null models.** Specifically, we establish four defensible contributions: (i) **null-model discipline on holiday network restructuring**, showing that although summer holidays sharply decouple sending cities from receiving destinations (reciprocity halving even at fixed edge count), ~93% of the shift in assortativity, rich-club compression, k-core promotion, and apparent non-contiguous diaspora communities are structural artefacts of the denser holiday degree sequence and spatial gravity, with genuine beyond-null topological reorganisation concentrated in second-order mixing ($R_{\mathrm{avg}}$); (ii) **a holiday cohort inversion hidden in pooled data**, demonstrating within a high-dimensional fixed-effects PPML and gravity-residual QAP design that pooled birthplace elasticities move little against ordinary February nights because two distinct migrations pull in opposite directions—pre-1981 rural-exodus arrivals and village-born residents surge inland in August, whereas recent urban-to-urban migrants dominate ordinary nights and Christmas Eve; (iii) **intergenerational village-level persistence** (in the upgraded *EPJ Data Science* package), pairing three-operator 7,974-municipality mobility networks with parental-birthplace microdata to show that strictly second-generation city-born ties sharpen in August down to the individual village level inside the same province; and (iv) **a dual housing mechanism for seasonal rural reversal**, quantifying that Spain's most depopulated zones flip from 24% to 99% net receivers on holiday nights via inherited/non-primary rural dwellings in summer (attenuating the emptying gradient by 48–65%, with 77% of inland cross-regional second homes located in a family's birth region) versus living relatives' primary homes on Christmas Eve (<2% attenuation).

### 1.3 Claims We Must NOT Make

To avoid immediate pushback from *EPJ Data Science* reviewers, the manuscript must explicitly **not** claim:

1. **First to compare holiday mobility networks with census migration matrices.** Taken by `li2016spring` (China Spring Festival vs 2010 *hukou* census via QAP). Position as extending `li2016spring` from 31 provinces and raw QAP to a sparse multi-thousand-node network with spatial/fixed-effects nulls, holiday-vs-ordinary contrasts, and a 50-year intergenerational exodus.
2. **First to document holiday-vs-ordinary structural reconfiguration or holiday rich clubs.** Taken by `wesolowski2017seasonal`, `li2020exploring`, `wen2026reframing`, `wei2018richclub`, and `zhu2021exploring`. Our novelty is testing those topological claims against degree-preserving rewiring (`zhou2007structural`) and showing most macro shifts are density/degree-forced.
3. **First to show from Spanish mobile-phone data that rural Spain refills in summer or experiences "return tourism".** Taken by `ine2020em1`, `osorio2024andalusia`, `garciagonzalez2024retorno`, `condecomelhorado2024uneven`, `garzon2026heritage`, and `osorio2026merindades`. Those studies are node-level, regional, single-origin, or infer return without birthplace links; we supply the national dyadic origin-to-destination test.
4. **First to show that migration stocks or social ties belong in gravity models of travel/tourism.** Taken by `cho2011friendship`, `etzo2014migration`, `dwyer2014migration`, `bailey2020europe`, `gavriilidis2021social`, `santanagallego2022tourism`, and `prietocuriel2024diaspora`. We extend this to *temporal holiday activation*, *arrival-cohort inversion*, and *village-scale domestic counter-flows*.
5. **First to show that density or degree sequence drives apparent network differences.** Well established mathematically (`park2003origin`, `zhou2007structural`, `vanwijk2010comparing`, `krings2012effects`, `fosdick2018configuring`, `cinelli2019generalized`, `cimini2019statistical`). Present this as applying rigorous null-model discipline to holiday mobility networks.
6. **Individual-level return, causal mediation, or unconditional pooled summer growth.** Aggregate OD flows cannot prove an individual returned to *their* ancestral village (ecological inference); cross-sectional non-primary housing regressions show *coefficient attenuation*, not causal mediation; coastal birthplace effects are *not detected* against February (not "zero"); and the pooled summer roots increase is baseline-dependent (significant vs October, not vs February) until decomposed by cohort.
7. **First to build an ML/Deep Gravity model for migration or seasonal mobility.** Taken by `simini2021deep`, `gu2024predicting`, `zhao2025effective`, and `cabanas2025human`. If we add a predictive benchmark, scope the claim strictly to testing whether *historical birthplace/cohort stocks* add out-of-sample lift on *holiday inland* flows beyond gravity/EIO/SCI baselines.

## 2. Threats: Papers Reviewers Could Use to Say "This Has Been Done"

Exhaustive searches across Europe, the US, China, East Asia (*Chuseok*, *Obon*), Southeast Asia (*Mudik*), and Africa uncovered **no paper** that links subnational mobile-phone holiday-vs-ordinary flows to historical birthplace/parental-origin stocks with spatial and topological nulls. However, eight specific papers or clusters represent genuine reviewer threats that must be pre-empted directly in the Introduction / Related Work:

1. **Threat 1 — `li2016spring` (China Spring Festival vs Census QAP).**
   - *Likely referee objection:* "Correlating big-data holiday networks with census migration matrices via QAP was already done in China ten years ago."
   - *How to position:* Credit `li2016spring` explicitly as the conceptual starting point, then list four decisive differences: (a) 31 provinces form a near-complete graph precluding topology analysis; (b) raw QAP omits distance and fixed effects, so geography inflates $r = 0.88$; (c) *hukou* captures active recent labour migrants rather than a 50-year-old exodus and its city-born second generation; (d) no ordinary-day baseline is tested.
2. **Threat 2 — `wen2026reframing` (*J. Transp. Geogr.* 2026) and `li2020exploring` (*J. Geogr. Sci.* 2020).**
   - *Likely referee objection:* "Wen et al. (2026) already compared regular vs holiday intercity mobility networks, showing macro backbone shifts, meso community reconfiguration, and rising cultural proximity / housing effects."
   - *How to position:* This is our **single newest direct comparator** and must be cited prominently. Differentiate sharply on three grounds: (a) Wen et al. measure *cultural proximity* and *housing prices* between prefecture cities without any census birthplace/migration matrix or rural villages; (b) neither Wen et al. nor Li et al. tests whether observed macro topological shifts survive degree-preserving rewiring or calibrated spatial nulls—our null models show that naive topological changes (assortativity, rich club, k-core, community fragmentation) can be largely density artefacts; (c) Spain's bimodal August pull (beach vs depopulated villages) creates a clean natural experiment between amenity tourism and ancestral return absent from *Chunyun* alone.
3. **Threat 3 — `wei2018richclub` (*Appl. Geogr.* 2018), `zhu2021exploring` (*Cities* 2021), and `kruse2025richclub` (arXiv 2025).**
   - *Likely referee objection:* "Rich-club structure in holiday migration networks and spatiotemporal mobility networks has already been analysed."
   - *How to position:* Cite all three and flip the critique into a methodological asset: whereas `wei2018richclub` and `zhu2021exploring` treat rich-club presence during *Chunyun* as a substantive finding, we demonstrate the `zhou2007structural` / `cinelli2019generalized` warning in action—comparing holiday against ordinary nights reveals that raw hub-to-hub density rises ($0.80 \to 0.96$) while the degree-normalised ratio collapses ($1.22 \to 1.05$) as city hubs saturate the graph.
4. **Threat 4 — `zhao2025effective` (*The Innovation* 2025) and `luan2024different` (*Humanit. Soc. Sci. Commun.* 2024).**
   - *Likely referee objection:* "Zhao et al. (2025) already modeled multi-holiday seasonal mobility across 400M phone users over 6 years and beat gravity/radiation; Luan et al. (2024) already showed homecoming breaks distance decay."
   - *How to position:* Both model seasonal mobility as a function of observed flow popularity ("effective distance") or nonlinear geographic distance *without* measuring where origin residents were born. Our work explains *why* effective distance shrinks selectively along specific city-to-village corridors on holidays—historical migration memory—and uses `luan2024different` to justify estimating night-specific nonlinear distance functions rather than assuming stationary friction.
5. **Threat 5 — `garzon2026heritage` (*Sustainability* 2026), `garciagonzalez2024retorno` (2024), `condecomelhorado2024uneven` (2024), `osorio2024andalusia` (*Sci. Rep.* 2024), and `ine2020em1`.**
   - *Likely referee objection:* "Spanish mobile-phone data have already been used repeatedly to show summer surges in rural/empty Spain and 'return tourism'."
   - *How to position:* Acknowledge this Spanish empirical baseline warmly, then point out the structural gap: `garzon2026heritage` reduces OD flows to node-level indicators; `garciagonzalez2024retorno` covers one region (Castilla-La Mancha) and *infers* return tourism from visitors minus hotel beds; `condecomelhorado2024uneven` tracks Madrid-only summer outflows; `osorio2024andalusia` covers Andalusia. None constructs a national OD network linked to census birthplaces, cohorts, or second-generation parental origins.
6. **Threat 6 — `bailey2018social`, `bailey2020europe` (*SocInfo* / `arXiv:2007.12177`), `gavriilidis2021social` (*Curr. Issues Tour.* 2021), `santanagallego2022tourism` (*Tour. Econ.* 2022), and `etzo2014migration` (*Ann. Tour. Res.* 2014).**
   - *Likely referee objection:* "Gravity models already establish that social ties (Meta SCI) and bilateral migration stocks predict rail travel and tourism."
   - *How to position:* These studies analyse static or annual aggregate flows (mostly international tourism or European inter-regional rail). None contrasts ordinary weekdays with specific holiday nights to isolate *holiday-specific activation*, none uncovers the *opposing cohort regimes* (pre-1981 exodus vs recent urban arrivals), and none pairs aggregate flows with village-level housing stocks and household second-home birthplace records. Running a direct horse race against Meta SCI (`_local/external/meta_sci_spain`) turns this threat into a showcase result.
7. **Threat 7 — `prietocuriel2024diaspora` (*PNAS Nexus* 2024) and `caballeroreina2024gravity` (2024).**
   - *Likely referee objection:* "The diaspora model already uses pre-existing migrant stocks to beat gravity at fine spatial scale."
   - *How to position:* `prietocuriel2024diaspora` models *permanent migration* drawn toward existing migrant diasporas at destinations. Our study documents the *inverse, half-century echo*: temporary circular holiday counter-flows from urban immigration hubs *back* toward depopulated origin provinces and villages long after net out-migration ended.
8. **Threat 8 — Mobile-phone bias and ecological fallacy (`pappalardo2021evaluation`, `verma2024comparison`, `dennijs2026tale`, `martinezbernabeu2025assessing`, `vanhoof2018assessing`).**
   - *Likely referee objection:* "EPJDS has shown that home-detection algorithms misclassify long-stay visitors, single-operator data suffer location-specific demographic bias, MITMA `_AM` zones aggregate villages, and city→province regressions are ecological."
   - *How to position:* Meet *EPJ Data Science*'s methodological standard head-on by citing its own bias papers (`pappalardo2021evaluation`, `verma2024comparison`, `dennijs2026tale`, `salgado2021end`), validating single-operator MITMA against three-operator INE municipal OD (`N = 7,974`, `r = 0.90–0.99` provincial, `ρ = 0.85–0.89` destination inflows; `n1_new_data.md`), incorporating ECEPOV 2021 city×village parental birthplaces + household second-home records (77.4% in family birth CCAA), and keeping all individual-level claims strictly hedged.

*(Audit note on non-Chinese homecoming holidays: dedicated searches for South Korea's Chuseok, Japan's Obon, Indonesia's Mudik, Italy's Ferragosto, and US SafeGraph/Meta Thanksgiving/Christmas mobility — including `chen2018effect` in *Science* on Thanksgiving smartphone family dinners — found no study linking subnational holiday OD flows to historical census birthplace or migration-stock matrices.)*

**Verdict on predictive novelty (Prompt Question 6: *"Would a 'roots improve holiday-flow prediction' result be new?"*):** **Yes, with an important empirical caveat.** Existing ML/Deep Gravity benchmarks (`simini2021deep`, `mauro2022generating`, `gu2024predicting`, `cabanas2025human`) feed *static contemporary node features* (land use, POIs, roads, GDP), and `zhao2025effective` relies on observed flow popularity; meanwhile, diaspora-stock predictive benchmarks (`prietocuriel2024diaspora`, `caballeroreina2024gravity`) predict *permanent migration*, not temporary holiday travel. No published paper tests whether adding **historical census birthplace/cohort stocks** improves out-of-sample prediction (CPC / Sørensen–Dice / Poisson deviance) specifically on **holiday versus ordinary nights** beyond gravity, radiation/EIO, and Meta SCI. **Caveat:** Because short-range and coastal leisure flows carry huge headcount in August, the *all-links* aggregate CPC improvement from roots could be small (~1–3 pp) even when the *inland long-range* or *rural-village* lift is large. ## 5. Framing Options for *EPJ Data Science*

Below are three distinct strategic framings tailored to *EPJ Data Science*'s editorial identity. **Option A is recommended as the primary frame**, incorporating Option B's null-model result as Section 4.1 and a compact stratified predictive check from Option C in Section 4.4 / Supplementary Information.

### Option A (Recommended): *"Digital traces uncover a hidden demographic memory"*
- **Pitch:** Fifty years after Spain's 1950–1975 rural exodus, national mobile-phone overnight networks uncover an intergenerational demographic memory invisible to population registers and hotel statistics. While macro holiday restructuring looks dominated by coastal hubs and is largely forced by denser degree sequences, dyadic origin-destination models reveal a sharp *cohort inversion*: August activates pre-1981 rural-exodus, village-born, and strictly second-generation parental ties inland through inherited family second homes ("la casa del pueblo", 48–65% attenuation), whereas ordinary nights and Christmas follow recent urban migrants and living kin (<2% housing attenuation).
- **Literatures spoken to:** Computational social science & digital-trace demography (`blondel2015survey`, `deville2014dynamic`, `salgado2021end`, `wesolowski2017seasonal`, `hoffmann2022measuring`), social/migration ties in mobility (`bailey2018social`, `cho2011friendship`, `santanagallego2022tourism`, `mulder2020young`), rural depopulation & homecoming (`collantes2014reducing`, `garciagonzalez2024retorno`, `galvezgarcia2025homecoming`, `masso2019generational`), plus disciplined network null models (`zhou2007structural`, `cimini2019statistical`).
- **Why it maximises acceptance at EPJDS:** Matches the exact profile of high-performing empirical EPJDS papers (e.g., `lavellehill2022using`, `hoffmann2022measuring`, `lucchini2025socioeconomic`): a memorable substantive discovery, rigorous multi-source validation (MITMA + 3-operator INE + Census + ECEPOV + Meta SCI), and honest null-model falsification of naive topological claims.
- **Main risk & mitigation:** Referees may ask "why should international readers care about Spanish villages?" Mitigate in paragraph 1 by positioning Spain as an ideal quantitative laboratory for a global post-urbanisation phenomenon (Italy's *aree interne*, France's *diagonale du vide*, Japan/Korea rural hollowing, and China's post-exodus trajectory as *hukou* migrants settle permanently in cities).

### Option B: *"Null models show holiday network restructuring is mostly density—the real shift is dyadic demographic memory"*
- **Pitch:** Recent studies report dramatic topological reorganisation of national mobility networks during holidays (assortativity, rich-club structure, core-periphery shifts, communities). Testing Spanish holiday versus ordinary networks against degree-preserving rewiring (`zhou2007structural`), $P(k)+\phi(k)$-preserving swaps (`zhou2017second`), and calibrated negative-binomial gravity nulls reveals that ~93% of assortativity change, rich-club compression, k-core village promotion, and apparent non-contiguous communities are *density/degree artefacts* (`vanwijk2010comparing`, `krings2012effects`, `cimini2019statistical`). True holiday-specific structure resides in second-order mixing ($R_{\mathrm{avg}}$) and in dyadic re-weighting toward historical rural-exodus roots.
- **Literatures spoken to:** Complex-network null models and structural constraints (`park2003origin`, `zhou2007structural`, `zhou2017second`, `vanwijk2010comparing`, `fosdick2018configuring`, `cinelli2019generalized`, `cimini2019statistical`, `expert2011uncovering`, `sarzynska2016null`) and holiday network topology (`wen2026reframing`, `wei2018richclub`, `zhu2021exploring`, `li2020exploring`, `schlosser2020covid`).
- **Why it could appeal to EPJDS:** Speaks directly to statistical-physics referees on the EPJDS editorial board who appreciate methodological cautions and well-calibrated falsifications.
- **Main risk & mitigation:** High risk of a "known theoretical result" dismissal ("we already knew degree sequence constrains assortativity and rich club since Park & Newman 2003 and Zhou & Mondragón 2007"), while relegating the paper's most original empirical discoveries (cohort inversion, 2nd-generation village persistence, summer vs Christmas housing duality) to secondary status. Better used as the opening structural pillar inside Option A.

### Option C: *"Historical roots as a missing memory layer in human mobility models"*
- **Pitch:** Classical and machine-learning spatial-interaction models (gravity, radiation, EIO, Deep Gravity) treat mobility as memoryless—driven by contemporary populations, distance, and land-use POIs. We show that historical migration stocks (birthplace cohorts and parental origins) constitute a latent, calendar-activated memory layer that explains why holiday distance decay breaks down (`luan2024different`, `zhao2025effective`) and improves out-of-sample flow prediction over gravity, EIO, and Meta SCI on holiday inland corridors.
- **Literatures spoken to:** Mobility laws and predictive flow benchmarking (`barbosa2018human`, `simini2012universal`, `lenormand2016systematic`, `simini2021deep`, `luca2022modeling`, `mauro2022generating`, `beiro2016predicting`, `prietocuriel2024diaspora`, `cabanas2025human`, `zhao2025effective`, `kang2026explaining`).
- **Why it could appeal to EPJDS:** Directly mirrors flagship *EPJ Data Science* modelling papers (`beiro2016predicting`, `luca2022modeling`, `mauro2022generating`, `kang2026explaining`) with crisp quantitative prediction benchmarks (CPC, log-RMSE).
- **Main risk & mitigation:** **High empirical risk.** If aggregate CPC improvement over a well-tuned doubly-constrained gravity or GBDT model is modest (e.g. +0.01–0.02 overall because high-volume urban/coastal corridors dominate national headcounts), modelling referees will judge the predictive gain too small for a modelling-first paper. Do not adopt Option C as the primary title/frame unless a pilot confirms a substantial, clean out-of-sample gain (especially on rural/inland holiday links).
