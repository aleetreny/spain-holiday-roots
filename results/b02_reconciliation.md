## Legacy rows not recomputed in b02

- `k_in_small_med / k_in_small_mean / in_str_small_med / kcore_small_mean`: village-level statistics; they belong to R1 (k-core promotion, wave 2). The legacy values used the buggy buffer coastal flag of p73/p92, so they must be recomputed with coastal_true there.
- `r_dir_in_out`: not in the b02 brief (only directed out-in is required).
- `r_pop_weighted`: weighted population mixing on the full matrix; not a binary basic property, not in the brief.
- `mean_km / share_100km / share_xprov / share_small_pct`: weighted reach statistics; not in the b02 brief.
