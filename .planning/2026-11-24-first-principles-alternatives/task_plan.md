# Task Plan: first-principles search for a better (simpler, network-native) research question

## Goal
Without anchoring on candidate A (1833 borders, reports/propuesta_final.md), find the question that scores highest on
clarity (general public), uniqueness (verified gap), wow, robustness, COMP0123 fit, publishability — any Spanish dataset.
Deliver reports/alternativas_desde_cero.md with a ranked shortlist, pilots with real numbers, and an honest comparison with A.

## First principles (what a 10 needs)
1. Clarity: one sentence a non-expert understands without a history/statistics lesson.
2. Network-native: the answer needs a network concept (hubs, clubs, communities, bridges, spreading, robustness, mixing),
   not a regression that merely uses OD data. COMP0123 rewards comparison of two networks / real vs randomised.
3. Uniqueness: question x data x method combination not published (check OpenAlex/Consensus/Exa/web, ES+EN).
4. Wow: non-obvious answer, one striking map/figure.
5. Robust: null models (rewiring, spatial configuration), replication across days/months.
6. Spain as natural laboratory: something extreme in Spain makes it the best place to answer a general question.
7. Feasible now (data on disk or quick download), 500-50k nodes, density < 5%.
